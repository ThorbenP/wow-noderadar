# v3.0.0

WoW Forever build 70862 stopped letting addons read the minimap the way NodeRadar 2.x
did, so the radar stayed empty. NodeRadar now reads the minimap in a new way.

## Changed

- **The minimap blinks now and then.** To read a node, NodeRadar moves the minimap
  under your mouse cursor for a moment — every few seconds, a little longer when a
  new node turns up. The rest of the time it stays where it is.
- **Nodes are read only while your mouse cursor rests on open world.** Turning the
  camera with a mouse button held, pointing at something in the world, or having the
  cursor on a window or bar pauses reading for that moment; your own mouseover is
  never interrupted.
- **Far fewer readings.** Every twenty seconds, and when a GatherMate2 spawn point
  comes into reach — never more often than every five seconds — a quick check covers
  your whole range; only a change costs more. A node you gather leaves the radar with
  your cast, and a node found again where one just vanished stays quiet.

## New

- **Faster with GatherMate2.** If GatherMate2 is installed, NodeRadar looks for a new
  node at its known spawn points first. It loads GatherMate2_Data by itself, so the
  newest data counts without importing it.
