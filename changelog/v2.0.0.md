# v2.0.0 — WoW Forever

NodeRadar now runs on **WoW Forever**. Classic Era and TBC Anniversary stay on 1.2.1.

## New

- **Reads your minimap directly** — no more flickering minimap, and every node your
  minimap shows appears on the radar, not only spawn points from a database.
- **No GatherMate2 needed** — NodeRadar knows all classic herbs and ores in every
  client language and learns new names when you gather them.
- **Sound** when a new node you can gather comes into range.
- **Required skill in the minimap tooltip**, coloured from red to grey.
- **Choose herbs and ores** — one checkbox each.
- **Hide nodes beyond your skill**, and **hide grey nodes** that give no skill-up.

## Changed

- Exact node positions with any minimap size or skin.
- The range rings follow your minimap's zoom, indoors and out, and are off by
  default.
- Settings are grouped into Radar, Which nodes, Hints and General.

## Removed

- The GatherMate2 requirement.
- The scan interval and combat pause settings — scanning is now invisible and costs
  almost nothing.
- The debug settings and the `/nr sweep`, `/nr minimap` and `/nr settle` commands.
