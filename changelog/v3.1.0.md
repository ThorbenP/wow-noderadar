# v3.1.0

## New

- **The minimap blink is almost gone.** NodeRadar still borrows your minimap for a
  split second to read it, but a stand-in now takes its place for that moment: the
  minimap's own terrain around you, with its frame or skin (SexyMap and others too),
  the buttons and pins of your addons, the node and group icons, your arrow and the
  coordinates. It fades back into the real minimap afterwards.
- **Nothing shows at your mouse cursor during a reading.** Quest areas, Blizzard's
  coordinates, the group finder's eye and frames other addons attach to the minimap
  hide while the minimap is away.
- **A one-time note after the update** tells you what changed.

## Fixed

- **A node on the minimap took up to 20 seconds to reach the radar.** NodeRadar looked
  for a node as soon as its spawn point reached the edge of the minimap at 233 yards,
  but the minimap shows a node only within about 185 yards — too early, and then not
  again until the next regular check. It now looks the moment the spawn point comes
  within 175 yards, so a node you ride towards reaches the radar about a second after
  it appears on the minimap.
- **A check waited after you steered with the mouse.** Once you let go of the mouse
  button, stop turning the camera or leave a tooltip, a due check now reads the
  minimap right away instead of two seconds later — so nodes show up even when you
  steer with short clicks.
- **Nodes you left behind lingered at the radar's edge.** A node farther away than the
  minimap shows (about 190 yards) now leaves the radar at once.
- **Lua errors with some addons installed.** An addon frame anchored to a line made
  NodeRadar fail while looking for frames attached to the minimap, and a protected
  frame under the cursor could raise an error about a secret value.

## Changed

- **A bigger, lighter radar for new installs.** The radar radius starts at 170 px
  instead of 120 px, node opacity at 60% instead of 100%. Your own settings stay as
  they are; *Restore defaults* applies the new values.
