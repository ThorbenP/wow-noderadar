# v3.0.2

## Fixed

- **Checks ran every five seconds** instead of every twenty while a spawn point was in
  range.

## Changed

- **Fewer blinks.** Nodes your filters hide — beyond your skill, without a skill-up, or
  unticked in the list — cost no further readings, and their spawn points no longer
  trigger a check. Where GatherMate2 knows no spawn point for a node you want, the
  minimap is checked once a minute instead of every twenty seconds. Changing a filter
  checks right away.
