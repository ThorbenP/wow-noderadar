# v1.0.1

Bugfix release.

## What changed

- **The scan pauses in combat.** Confirming a node means moving the minimap under
  the cursor for a frame per sample, which took the minimap and the mouse focus
  away in the middle of a fight. Combat now counts as "cannot look", the same as a
  held mouse button: sampling stops, hit expiry pauses with it, and the radar holds
  what it last confirmed until the fight ends instead of emptying itself.
