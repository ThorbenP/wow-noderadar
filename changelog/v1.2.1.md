# v1.2.1

## Fixed

- **The minimap's own icons no longer flash during a scan.** The client draws mail,
  meeting stones and similar markers from a texture of their own, and that one was
  missing from the list the scan blanks — five of the six it offers were covered, this
  one was not. Party and raid blips still show: this client has no
  `SetClassBlipTexture`, and they are drawn where no addon reaches them.
