# v3.0.1

## Fixed

- **Nodes no longer come and go.** Right after the minimap changed size for a
  reading, the next reading saw nothing. Nodes on your minimap were missed, nodes
  just found left the radar again, and searches failed and took long. Each reading
  now waits the moment the game needs to draw the resized minimap.

## Changed

- **The description explains the blinking minimap** — why Blizzard's update makes it
  necessary, when it happens, and when NodeRadar waits instead.
- **Setting up GatherMate2 is described step by step**, including importing
  GatherMate2_Data into GatherMate2 once.
